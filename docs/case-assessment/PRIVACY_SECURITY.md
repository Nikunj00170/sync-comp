# Privacy and security note

**Scope:** synthetic Project Udyam Digital prototype, not a claim of legal certification. All fixture businesses, contacts and identifiers are fictional. Treat uploads and account records as private nonetheless.

**Purpose and consent.** Collect business needs to recommend catalogue products; collect registration/address/signatory evidence only for business onboarding. KYC consent is explicit and separate from optional AI conversation consent. Explain OCR, synthetic registry checks and the lack of live identity verification before approval. Changed uploads or relevant profile details revoke consent/approval. Removing files withdraws those documents; consent can also be revoked without deleting them. Customer-authenticated POST lookups use one identifier, never bulk data.

**Minimization.** The browser receives masked registry identifiers and issue codes, not raw OCR text or full registries. OCR cache and historical lead files are server/development inputs only. GenAI receives needs, compact catalogue and bounded conversation context, never KYC uploads or access tokens. Voice additionally uses Sarvam after microphone consent. Send no identity documents, bank details or passwords in chat. Provider retention is subject to the provider agreement and must be agreed before using real personal data.

**Access and security.** Cognito PKCE access JWTs bind records to `sub`; server validation checks issuer/client/token use/expiry. Private storage keys are generated on the server. Signed uploads expire in five minutes, prohibit overwrite and are verified on completion; per-account quotas and conditional transactions prevent substitution and stale approvals. API totals are authoritative; checkout is idempotent. Logs should contain operational status/request IDs, never tokens, OCR text or full identifiers. Real deployment needs least-privilege roles, HTTPS, private buckets, encryption, throttling, monitoring and log access controls. The local AI gateway is not suitable for direct public exposure.

**Retention and deletion.** Local demo keeps metadata on the device until reset; memory-mode backend bytes disappear on restart. In S3 mode, deleting an owned document invalidates KYC before deleting the object; a failed deletion is retryable. There is currently no automatic cloud-retention job or applied S3 lifecycle policy. Before accepting real documents, configure an approved retention period (prototype recommendation: delete abandoned KYC objects after 30 days), expire noncurrent versions if versioning is enabled, set log retention, and implement account erasure/reconciliation of metadata, backups and audit obligations. Do not advertise automatic deletion until that policy is deployed and verified. Transient advisor sessions expire after 30 minutes and are explicitly deleted on clear/close; browser history is limited to 12 turns. Synthetic case data and cached specimen OCR remain in the repository for reproducibility.

**Human review.** Unreadable, mismatched, inactive or stale documents require review. Mock approval is labelled demo and cannot activate a real service. Live DigiLocker, e-sign and legal identity decisions require provider agreements, approved workflows and appropriate privacy review.

Browser-only demo payment receipts include the simulated reference, amount and business/address snapshot, scoped to the signed-in subject on this device. They do not store payment credentials or change server payment records; clear browser site data to remove them.
